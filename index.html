Interactive · Robot Arm Lab

Can you move the robot arm to the target?

A three-joint arm with fixed links. Work out the joint angles that put its gripper on the gold target, then press Go and see how close you get.

  1. Read the target x and y, and the link lengths L1, L2, L3.
  2. Calculate/guess θ1, θ2, θ3 in degrees. θ1 is measured from the x-axis; θ2 and θ3 are relative to the previous link. Anticlockwise is positive.
  3. Enter them and press Go. The arm moves from its home position (all angles 0°).
  4. You get 3 tries. Your result shows after the third. Then reset for a new target.

    The Robotics Society, India

    A national society of researchers, industrialists and students working in robotics. TRS Local Chapter – Gujarat is its first local chapter.

    Who we are

    Bringing India’s robotics researchers together

    The society was formed to grow robotics activity in India and to strengthen interaction among researchers, as the field expanded into education, rehabilitation, entertainment and home applications.

    Older documents refer to it as the Robotics Society of India (RSI). Its registered name is The Robotics Society.

    Visit rs-india.org (opens in a new tab)

    What TRS does

    • Advances in Robotics (AIR)The society’s international conference, held every two years around July at leading institutes.
    • Thematic workshopsHeld in the years between AIR editions, on themes such as field robotics, intelligent systems and robotics in industry.
    • Student chaptersThe TRS-SCALE programme funds activities run by student chapters at member institutions.
    • Local chaptersLocal chapters such as TRS Local Chapter – Gujarat build networks in their region.
    • Newsletter and alliancesA periodic newsletter, and an alliance with the Association for Machines and Mechanisms (AMM) and the Indian Society of Mechanical Engineering (ISME).

    Inauguration of TRS Local Chapter – Gujarat

    • Saturday 22 August 2026 · 11:00 AM
    • Birla Vishvakarma Mahavidyalaya Engineering College, Vallabh Vidyanagar

    The chapter was formally launched with a lamp-lighting ceremony, marking the establishment of the first local chapter of TRS in India. Distinguished experts from academia, research and industry attended, including representatives from TRS, BARC and leading institutions across Gujarat.

    The event was graced by a special visit from Dr. Debanik Roy, Scientist at BARC and former Vice President of TRS. Dr. Ekta Singla, Secretary of The Robotics Society, guided and encouraged the initiative throughout. The first office bearers of the chapter were announced at the event.

    How it works. Five infrared sensors read how far the line is from the robot’s centre. The controller steers in proportion to that error (Kp) and to how fast it is changing (Kd). Too little steering and the robot drifts off on curves; too much, without damping, and it wobbles. Speed is in km/h and travel time is worked out from the straight-line distance to the venue, so the map runs as a time-lapse. Best runs and completed cities are saved on this device.
